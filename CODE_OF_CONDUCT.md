# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be considerate, assume good
intent, critique ideas rather than people, respect privacy, and accept constructive
feedback.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, publishing
private information, deliberate disruption, or other conduct inappropriate in a
professional community.

## Enforcement

Report community concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile.
Maintainers may remove content or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
behavior violates this standard. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
